Druid, Ranger
_1st-level Transmutation_ 

**Casting:** 1 action (V, S, M - ball of sap) 

**Range:** 60 feet

**Duration:** Instant  

Make a ranged spell attack against a creature within range. On hit the target takes 2d6 acid damage and becomes restrained until the end of its next turn. 

**Empower (+1):** The acid damage increases by 1d6.
